# Implementation preregistration — before diagnostic fitting

Scope: retrospective prediction of supplied processed fluorescence, not raw optical observations, causal online forecasts, voltage or conductances. Existing six-file protocol checksum manifest, native identity receipt, training split and previous NEXT_EXPERIMENT are authoritative. No validation/test recording is opened by the modelling loader. No full sheaf or multiscale metric will be computed.

Frozen circuit/pairs/history/grid: all ten unordered training-supported pairs in the fixed twelve-neuron circuit; both directions; primary ten prior samples and sensitivity five/fifteen; alpha0.1/1/10/100; nested leave-one-animal-out inside training15. Each complete pair needs≥3 training animals. Day sensitivity replaces outer and inner animal folds by date groups, restricted to the same training animals; at least three distinct dates are needed for nested date folds. Unsupported date analyses are NOT EVALUABLE; no split moves or substitute pairs.

At target index t the design uses [t−1,…,t−H] for each neuron and each behavioural channel. Behavioural channels: signed velocity, absolute speed, reversal state, author-provided radial distance, pumping. Including signed velocity and speed strengthens the permitted prior-behaviour baseline without using heading signs, future behaviour or behavioural outcomes. All models share the identical pair/behaviour finite-window mask and continuous segments. Persistence predicts y_j(t−1). Neural timestamp gaps use the unchanged1.5×median rule; all ordinary source segments are verified. Every history length is reported; none selected by outer performance.

Fold normalisation: equal-animal mean and variance of training design columns and targets only; constant columns receive scale1. No supplied whole-record z-scores or per-held-out-animal centring. All available windows within a fitting animal have equal weight, and each animal has equal total weight. Ridge minimises balanced sum squared error plus alpha times coefficient squared norm; intercept unpenalised. Equivalently mean animal MSE plus alpha/N_training_windows penalty. This fixes the previously unspecified objective convention before fits. Inner selection minimises equal-animal training-scale-normalised MSE, separately per receiver for unrestricted models; exact ties prefer larger alpha. Original-unit and training-scale-normalised outer MSE are both reported.

Models: persistence; receiver-only ridge AR; ridge bivariate VAR; receiver-history + behavioural-history ridge; VAR + behavioural-history ridge; rank-one cross-lag bivariate ridge; independently anatomical-edge-constrained ridge VAR. The rank-one model has separate unrestricted own-history filters and off-diagonal lag matrix C∈R^(2×H) with rank≤1: the two cross-direction filters share a temporal kernel with different signed gains. It does not treat a single receiver coefficient vector's tautological rank as a low-rank constraint. Deterministic SVD initialisation from unrestricted training ridge, followed by alternating exact ridge updates of own-filters/gains and the shared kernel; max200 iterations, relative objective tolerance1e−8. Penalise the actual filter coefficients, not arbitrary factor scaling. Kernel norm is fixed to1 after each cycle without changing the represented filters. Record convergence; never remove poorly converged folds. Select rank-one alpha jointly by the mean of its two receiver inner normalised MSEs to preserve one coherent pair model. Report that joint tuning difference explicitly.

The anatomical comparator uses the already-local White composite reference TSV, pinned by SHA256 before fitting. Chemical edges follow listed direction; electrical rows permit both directions; counts only indicate structural support, not conductance. If an edge is not listed the comparator omits its cross-history, without claiming biological absence. No adjacency is inferred from correlation, activity or held-out response. Source mismatch/incomplete anatomy remains a limitation. Graph comparator is reported, never used to choose eligible pairs.

Outer predictions are scored per animal, never per time point as independent replicates. Training/apparent MSE is separately recorded from outer generalisation. For each direction, Δ=100×(1−mean_animal MSE_augmented/mean_animal MSE_baseline). Primary conditional contrast is VAR+behaviour versus AR+behaviour; unconditioned contrast VAR versus AR. Report raw and normalised versions, individual-animal sign counts, and leave-one-animal-out evaluation influence ranges. Comparators include rank-one/graph versus AR and persistence contrasts. No favourable pair is dropped.

Uncertainty:10,000 percentile paired bootstrap resamples at the animal level, seed20261102; all panels/directions/windows belonging to a sampled animal travel together. Per-direction intervals have only3–6 independent units and are descriptive, unadjusted20-direction intervals, not confirmatory discoveries. Overall contrast first averages supported normalised losses over all directions within each animal, then weights animals equally; shared receiver outcomes in overlapping panels do not create extra animals. Bootstrap all animals together for the pooled interval. Day sensitivity additionally reports date-cluster intervals. These are conditional-on-cross-validation-predictions intervals and do not refit the nested algorithm for every bootstrap. Report that limitation. No window/cell bootstrap or misleading pair-independent pooled interval.

Proceed only toward a separately preregistered multivariate missing-state experiment if conditional improvements are consistent across animals, lags and date sensitivity; this diagnostic cannot validate a sheaf or its higher-order benefit. Weak pairwise results do not rule out collective interactions. Future≥5% threshold and uncertainty criteria are unchanged; no performance-dependent changes, validation/test unlock, circuit expansion or compatibility metrics are permitted.
