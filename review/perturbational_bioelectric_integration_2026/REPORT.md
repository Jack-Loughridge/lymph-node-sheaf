## What the focused screen established

**1. Cahill astrocytes — strongest published spatial local-input/network design; compact-export branch insufficient.** The public manifest identifies `events.zip` (61.97MB), exclusions and a relevant12.01GB MATLAB object. Independent groups are4 WT,8 genetic-Cx43 and4 CBX mice, with multiple slices/FOVs and sequential neurotransmitter trials nested within them. These are not121 independent networks for inference. [Dryad data/README](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.83bk3jb0j)

Six actual author code files were retrieved and verified against pinned Git blobs. The [export-schema audit](AUTHOR_EXPORT_SCHEMA_AUDIT.json) establishes that the documented compact representation exports timed event features and event-to-cell membership, but no continuous fluorescence or complete observed-cell roster. It cannot distinguish a zero-event cell from an unobserved cell; exported centroids describe event footprints rather than fixed cell ROIs, and stimulation-frame presets are not the measured laser log. This finding concerns the documented exporter, not a byte-level inspection of the blocked ZIP or proof that the large MAT lacks necessary fields. [Author extraction code](https://github.com/poskanzerlab/astro-network-encoding/blob/v1.0/python/conversion/aqua_extract.m)

The paper reports focal input, neighbouring responses, genetic reduction, protein validation and laser/receptor controls. Cx43 dependence does not uniquely identify electrical conductance; calcium and second-messenger mechanisms remain relevant. A compact per-FOV trace/ROI/pulse export is the appropriate next step, not downloading12GB to discover its schema. The4-mouse WT group is below the planning target; no lower-variance/pairing justification is established. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07311-5)

**2. Rühl voltage — strongest small public payload to inspect next, not yet a usable network.** Genetic Cx43/Cx45 double knockout and Cx43 re-expression provide a more specific comparison than a blocker alone. Relative voltage is suitable; absolute calibration is not required. The paper reports3–6 independent dishes, not50 independent cultures when50 traces are overlaid. Trace count does not establish simultaneous coverage. Methods identify background/bleach corrections, initial cropping and some binning; randomisation/blinding were not used. [Primary methods and Source Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76758-z?error=cookies_not_supported)

The exact Source Data ZIP link resolves as a binary object, but its bytes, internal files, dish/session joins, geometry and local-drive metadata have **not** been inspected. Its size remains unknown. Optical correlation analysis is not a direct conductance measurement; we have not verified a selective local-input response outside the driven cell. This is a potentially useful voltage-stability benchmark, not yet a multiregion propagation dataset. Raw images are author-request-only; they are not initially needed if traces and metadata suffice. One≤50MB manual transfer can settle the public-file uncertainty.

**3. Elvers muscle voltage — strongest mechanism-focused author request; a smaller-system exception is plausible.** The study reports simultaneously observed three-muscle ensembles, genetic innexin perturbations and cell-specific optical clamp. The clamp cohort has13 WT,20 unc-9,15 Cx36 and14 inx-11 animals, which meets the planning target on paper. However, the documented local-clamp assay observes **two cells**, while the three-cell recordings concern spontaneous activity. Those assays cannot be joined into a measured three-cell perturbation trajectory. The data statement promises future numerical release; only two representative movies are linked. [Primary reviewed preprint](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/111279v1)

A two-cell system can qualify as the expressly allowed smaller-system physiological benchmark; neither twenty cells nor absolute voltage is a universal gate. Local physiology is promising, but numeric cohort access is not verified. Ask whether the camera captured a usable third cell during the same clamp. If not, that multiregion extension requires a new measurement. Locomotion is an independently reported phenotype, not established as a same-animal prediction target. Cx36 overexpression is not an unc-9 rescue, and more synchrony is not assumed healthier. Full source fields are listed in [author-request drafts](AUTHOR_REQUESTS.md); no messages were sent.

**4. Endothelial Cx43 — functional outcome strength, public matrix uncertainty.** Yale lists nine replication datasets/85 files, including small per-mouse quantification workbooks and functional assay data. The main genotype-comparison manifest exposes three control and three cKO mouse files. A workbook's name is not evidence for or against hidden time series; its bytes were not accessible here. An ingested one-variable table must not be mistaken for the original multi-sheet workbook. Nifedipine compensation is not restoration of Cx43 conductance. [Public study/catalog](https://dataverse.yale.edu/dataverse/skinendothelialcalcium), [genotype file manifest](https://doi.org/10.60600/YU/BO10R3)

**5. Ammer ShakB — access and simultaneous grouping unresolved.** The study combines genetic manipulation with voltage/calcium physiology and visual outcomes. Some calcium somata can be co-imaged; separately patched neurons must not be counted as a simultaneous network. GIN access/manifest inspection failed, so no filenames, sizes, independent fly count or regional cohort reconstruction is claimed. This is NOT EVALUABLE, not proof of absent data. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.03.040), [advertised repository](https://gin.g-node.org/gammer/Ammer_et_al_2022.git)

**6. Choi retina — stop the documented public representation.** Its small Excel deposit includes WT whole-retina time courses, integrated spatial grids, mutant propagation summaries and morphology. Those fields do not establish matched mutant/control cellular state trajectories. A morphology endpoint is valuable but cannot reconstruct missing dynamics. The rejection applies to the documented deposited representation, not to uninspected original microscopy or unpublished files. [Author file descriptions](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.1g1jwsv9d)

**7. Gao osmotic waves — stop at mechanism/control gate.** The deposit lists representative calcium videos, not a complete independent-culture trace/metadata cohort. Gap27/18α-GA effects do not independently establish selective coupling disruption, and RyR2 knockdown targets intracellular release rather than the junction. Broad osmotic exposure also complicates local influence. More segmentation of these examples cannot fix missing specificity/replication. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70094), [public clip manifest](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.djh9w0wbg)

**8. Beta/delta islets — a justified additional lead, not acquired.** Delta-selective Cx36 deletion and paracrine blockade offer distinct mechanistic tests with simultaneous cell-type calcium measurements reported. The processed numeric array/animal-islet-spatial manifest was not verified; the cited algorithm repository redirects through an unsupported connector endpoint. No claim of data absence or independence from cells alone follows. Stop access exploration pending a canonical processed location. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2504151122)
