Frozen search and audit protocol, 2026-10-31
Scope: international cross-organism discovery; no pilot edits or disease modelling.
Benchmark: original acceptance document, hash recorded in INITIAL_INPUTS.json.
Discovery target >=12 candidates; deep source/file audit >=5; real small-file
inspection for up to 3 feasible finalists before considering predictive tests.
Rank by intervention-to-spatial receiver match, measured resolution/coverage,
independent replication, public usable processed files, then anatomical reach.
Do not count cells/sections as animals or cultures. Do not treat inferred spatial
positions, deconvolution or imputed transcriptomes as measured cell observations.
At least two mechanistically distinct programmes; >=10 response variables and
>=5 detected in >=20% correctly typed receivers; no input-target reuse.
Required counts/fractions, 5% held-out gains, donor uncertainty and stability
thresholds remain those in the fixed benchmark. Physiological responses require
a declared measurement model; a single readout does not silently replace ten
independent response variables. Pathway hypotheses are feasibility candidates,
not post-hoc selected predictive successes.
No prediction until actual annotations, feature detection, spatial units, sample
IDs, complete mechanisms and external response maps support resolution/coverage.
If eligible, freeze dataset-specific maps/grid and train/test units before test
outcomes. Fit normalization/calibration only in training units. Compare intrinsic
target-excluded baseline and geometry/type-preserving null; bootstrap animals.
Stop a candidate after a structural mismatch or inaccessible mandatory files;
record uncertainty separately from biological failure. No indefinite rescue.
Prefer metadata/panels/small processed tables. Cap any initial payload at 50 MB;
inspect a manifest/size before transfer, retain payloads only under ignored data/.
Record retrieval failure without concluding the source is absent/private.
Do not export observations, push GitHub, modify microbiome, classify disease,
fit Alpha-learning or perform pushforwards. Report their future requirements.
