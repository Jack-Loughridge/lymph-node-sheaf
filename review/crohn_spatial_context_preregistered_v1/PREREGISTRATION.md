# Spatial context → stromal response: separate prospective feasibility protocol

Status: **PREREGISTERED, NOT FITTED; EXTERNAL INPUTS BLOCKED**. Date: 2026-11-02. This is a timestamped repository protocol, not a claim of registration with an external registry. No spatial cytokine outcome scores or predictive results have been inspected for this representation. Existing receptor-coverage results and panel composition are known; they motivated this question. No claim of fully data-naive design is made.

The original biological criteria and Crohn NO-GO are immutable. This protocol defines a different estimand; success would not retrospectively pass the original per-cell receptor/exposure gate. No original report or threshold may be overwritten. The frozen original-file hashes and this protocol's own hash distinguish the two experiments.

## 1. Question, units and estimand

For an independently supported broad intestinal fibroblast population, does measured local ligand-source context predict an externally defined cytokine-response programme in a patient not used for training, beyond receiver state, surrounding cell composition and independently annotated anatomy?

The estimand is the **patient-weighted incremental out-of-sample reduction in response-score mean squared error** from adding local ligand context. This is prediction of spatially associated transcriptional programmes, not a cytokine causal effect, secretion measurement, receptor occupancy or identification of direct cell–cell restriction maps. Patient, not cell or tissue, is the independent unit. Both tissues of a patient remain in the same fold. Affected/adjacent labels are descriptive, never training features or dictionary selection criteria. Adjacent is not healthy.

Primary programmes, fixed now: IFNG, TNF and IL1B. OSM remains excluded from primary analysis. At least two independently supported programmes must pass. Overlap of TNF and IL1B targets and common NF-kB biology must be quantified; two names alone do not establish two independently predictable mechanisms.

## 2. Mandatory data gates before fitting

1. Obtain authentic S1, three cytokine-vs-unstimulated DEG tables and GSE307526 replicate expression/condition metadata. Hash source files; inspect gene identifiers, contrast direction, scale, sample labels, dose, time, treatment and control replication. Do not infer replicate assignments from column order or treat normalized values as raw counts. Biological replicates versus repeat cultures must be explicitly distinguished. Four cultures are not four spatial patients.
2. Verify all 16 tissue identifiers against S1 and the private workbook. Resolve shared-slide patients by complete specimen/biobank information and SCP order. Unresolved mapping blocks all patient folds. Report S1 precedence and discrepancies privately. Eight independent paired patients must be verified; no seven-slide analysis.
3. Use raw-count QC fixed previously (>=100 panel counts, >=20 detected genes, finite centroid and positive area), without requiring receiver receptor-mRNA detection. Broad fibroblast identity must have independent support sufficient for the existing >=90% broad and >=70% receiver-subtype gates. Fine inflammatory state labels are excluded from primary predictors and eligibility: these may encode the outcome itself. Author broad labels and target-excluded marker corroboration must be audited; if unavailable, any label-proxy result is exploratory and the criterion remains NOT EVALUABLE. A target-excluded, externally referenced fibroblast identity check is required for confirmation. Do not reuse outcome genes to annotate receivers.
4. Freeze a competence ledger **by broad receiver type and programme**, independent of this atlas's response scores: human intestinal fibroblast cytokine perturbation evidence (prefer primary cells), all canonical receptor components supported in that population by independent RNA/protein evidence, and evidence applicable to the same tissue/type. Record source, species, cell line versus primary, dose/time and evidence tier. The CCD18-Co line can support plausibility, not establish every in-situ subtype's competence. Mixture responses cannot prove each component's effect. No competence entry may be inferred from the tested response outcome or invented for an unmeasured complex. Incomplete support means NOT EVALUABLE, not assumed competence. Do not require every receiver to have detectable receptor mRNA; measured receptor counts remain descriptive covariates only, not eligibility gates or primary predictors.
5. Response and context support must pass sections 3–4. Obtain independently drawn histological/anatomical regions or an independent, target-excluded anatomical reference; source CellCharter clusters and outcome-enriched labels do not satisfy this requirement. If only computed expression neighbourhoods exist, report an exploratory region surrogate and do not claim the anatomical-confounding gate passed.
6. Verify the number of run-specific negative-control probe targets and relevant count universe using gene_panel.json, feature metadata or analysis_summary.html. Needed control profiles are the small negative-control rows from cell_feature_matrix.h5 (or MEX features+control rows), or equivalent per-control exports. Whole raw microscopy and full transcript-cloud downloads are not required. Aggregate-only estimates may support a sensitivity analysis, but cannot establish per-probe gene-specific calibration.

Failure of an access/provenance gate leaves this representation **BLOCKED / NOT EVALUABLE**, not scientifically disproved. Do not run a substitute fit using guessed signatures, S1 or anatomy.

## 3. External response dictionary, fixed without spatial outcomes

For each named single cytokine versus its actual unstimulated control, retain measured genes with adjusted P<0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >=1. Require >=10 non-input response genes on the panel, and >=5 detected in >=20% eligible receivers. This detection check is an outcome-blind coverage gate across all tissues, never a ranking by held-out association. Report tissue-level and patient-level coverage after mapping verification.

Exclude the union of all primary ligand and receptor genes, all genes actually used for receiver annotation or anatomical assignment, and all other pathways' response genes from model inputs. The outcome union must never enter receiver baselines, feature selection, normalisation-size factors or region construction. Do not substitute H5AD cell-state DEGs for cytokine perturbation DEGs. Use all eligible external genes with equal signed weights, sign(log2 fold change); no spatial feature ranking or target-performance filtering. Freeze the dictionary, metadata, exclusions and hashes before constructing any spatial response scores.

Inspect culture replicate reproducibility: contrast direction in each leave-one-culture-out comparison and bootstrap 95% effect intervals where replicate units and scale support it. Preserve the published adjusted significance estimates; do not re-run DESeq2 on normalized values. If counts or replicate identities do not permit reproducibility checks, state NOT EVALUABLE. A line response is not matched-donor organoid validation. Require at least two programmes with genuine experimental support and distinct informative targets; report Jaccard overlap and effect correlation. Also evaluate disjoint-target scores by excluding genes shared across primary dictionaries, with the same >=10/>=5 coverage gates. If distinct signals cannot be tested, programme-specific interpretation is NOT EVALUABLE even if an aggregate inflammatory score predicts well.

For receiver i, response Y_ip = mean_g[sign(beta_pg) Z_ig]. Z is centered/scaled log1p expression, with gene location/scale learned from training patients only (equal patient weights). Expression size factor uses a target/input/annotation-excluded reference-gene set: detected >=20% of training receivers, training median reference count per cell >0; normalise counts to 10,000 relative to that set's cell total, zero reference-total receivers rejected with counts reported. The same frozen reference set applies to test cells; no test-distribution fitting. Response scoring and intrinsic predictors must share a documented target-excluded size-factor rule; no denominator containing response genes. Fit-free alternative raw-count pseudobulk checks are sensitivity only.

## 4. Ligand context and background

For each tissue separately, summarize QC immune sender cells (B/plasma, myeloid, T/NK) around each receiver; no self edges or cross-section neighbours. Primary radius is 2.5 tissue median segmentation-equivalent diameters; 1.5 and 4 are prespecified sensitivity radii. Distances use source micrometres. These ranges describe local measured context, not the maximum diffusion distance or a physical cytokine transport law. No alternate larger radius may be added to rescue failure.

Use a compact triangular kernel K(d)=max(0,1-d/r). A ligand-source feature is log1p(sum_s K(d_is) C_sp / max(sum_s K(d_is) A_s, epsilon)), with A_s sender segmented area and C_sp raw ligand count. Fixed epsilon=1 square micrometre; empty neighbourhoods get feature zero plus an explicit empty-context indicator. Accompany this with unweighted ligand sum, sender count, density and lineage fractions for interpretation; ligand feature sets are fixed and not selected from outcome associations. Baselines receive the same geometry, density and composition variables.

Background calibration uses **probe controls only**, normalized by the verified number of negative-control probe targets; codeword counts are separate decoding QC. Estimate tissue-level mean per-control-per-cell and a control-derived context expectation using the identical sender membership and kernel. Required probe rows permit treating each control target as a pseudo-ligand; compare observed ligand-context statistic to empirical control-context statistics, preserving spatial structure and exposure area. Report control heterogeneity; an aggregate scalar cannot reconstruct this empirical distribution. Set the prespecified support flag above the 99th percentile of these control-context statistics; this is a study-defined flag, not a 10x gene positivity rule. Use all continuous contexts, including below-support values, for the primary prediction; support flags only define prespecified reporting strata. No adaptive two-count per-cell ligand or receptor cutoff.

Per programme require >=100 above-control supported receiver contexts across >=3 independently mapped patients and >=20% of eligible receivers in each of >=3 patients. This new representation's context-support gate **does not replace or pass original receptor-competent exposure C1**. Missing reliable background support is NOT EVALUABLE. Estimate false-positive burden under a verified denominator; conditional 20-control estimates are not verified calls. Never call nonzero ligand transcripts secreted protein.

Restrict primary evaluation to receiver centres >=r from the observed tissue coverage boundary, using independently provided section outlines. Holes/tears count as boundaries. Without reliable outlines, assess a conservative cell-cloud boundary sensitivity but do not claim full edge control or calibrated exposure. Report retained/rejected counts and possible censoring; no FOV or patient selection based on effects.

## 5. Mathematical representation and limits

Let q_i be broad receiver type, h_i independently measured anatomy, z_i intrinsic target-excluded state and c_ip measured local ligand context. Define m0_p(z_i,q_i,h_i,composition_i,geometry_i) and an added context term b_p c_ip, fitted only on training patients. The prospective compatibility relation is

Y_ip = m0_p(...) + b_p c_ip + error_ip.

For fixed learned coefficients and covariance, a residual Y_ip - m0_p - b_p c_ip could define an affine **context-to-state factor** with training-calibrated uncertainty. Context c_ip is a weighted aggregate of measured source variables, so the factor couples a receiver to a neighbourhood, not a verified direct sender–receiver synapse-like edge. Its domain is a tissue-context/receiver-state factor graph or hypergraph. Intercepts require affine constraints; they cannot silently be treated as a homogeneous linear cellular sheaf. If a later sheaf is justified, use explicit homogeneous augmentation or an affine formulation with units/weights declared. No sheaf is constructed here and residual minimisation cannot validate the factor.

The tested estimand averages latent prior exposures, unmeasured cytokines, migration and epigenetic memory. Snapshot data cannot identify unique direct couplings, causality, time delays or whether inflammatory states are healthy. A predictive pass would support only context-associated response compatibility, not current ligand dependence or original mechanistic gate success. Shared intracellular pathways may yield non-identifiable programme coefficients; report conditioning/collinearity rather than assign causal mechanisms.

## 6. Baselines and leakage control

All models use identical training/test receiver sets and patient weights. Models are ridge linear regression (interpretable primary) and a prespecified target-excluded gradient-boosted-tree receiver baseline (strong nonlinear challenger; 200 trees, maximum depth 3, learning rate .05, squared loss, fixed seed 20261102). Ridge regularisation grid [0.1,1,10,100]; standardisation, missing-value imputation, PCA if needed and feature selection fit training-only. Intrinsic gene features are all permitted panel genes detected in >=20% training receivers, reduced to <=30 training PCA components. No outcome/input gene appears in intrinsic features. All unsupervised learned transforms are also training-only. No patient-ID predictors; no disease-status predictor.

B0: mean and broad-type intercept for calibration diagnostics only, insufficient for success.
B1: strongest of ridge/nonlinear receiver-intrinsic predictors, plus target-excluded reference counts, area and control-quality covariates; total_counts containing outcome genes is prohibited as a predictor.
B2: B1 plus local cell-type composition, total density, sender area and geometry.
B3: B2 plus independently measured anatomical region; test-region categories unseen in training use a declared unknown indicator. Independent anatomy cannot be replaced by outcome-derived CellCharter.
B4: B3 plus per-tissue global immune ligand abundance (to test local versus section-wide inflammation).
Full: B4 plus the prespecified local ligand context. Full must add value beyond B1, B2, B3 and B4, and matched nulls; no weak-baseline substitution. Each baseline uses its own training-selected regularisation. To avoid handicap, fit the same ridge/tree families with and without context and compare training-selected best families; tree depth/count/rate are fixed. Simpler ligand-only and composition-only models are descriptive comparators.

Keep stromal inflammatory subtype labels, spatial clusters constructed from response genes, latent embeddings fitted with outcomes and outcome-gene PCs out of all predictors. A target-excluded reannotation sensitivity is mandatory if original broad labels used target genes. No predicted response is used to choose cells or regions.

## 7. Held-out design and matched nulls

Outer leave-one-patient-pair-out over all eight verified patients. Inner leave-one-training-patient-out chooses ridge regularisation/family using equal-patient MSE; primary radius remains fixed, not selected. Both tissues stay together at every split. Each patient's loss averages its available paired tissues equally, then receivers equally within tissue; pooled-cell MSE is secondary. No best-FOV selection. Freeze all inputs, dictionary and model configuration before opening held-out scores. Run the outer grid once and retain every prediction privately, including failures.

Generate 100 fixed-seed null contexts before scoring, independently within each tissue, broad sender lineage and independent anatomical region. Null 1 permutes ligand profiles among matched source cells within density and non-target library-size quintiles, preserving cell types, positions and composition but not necessarily ligand autocorrelation. Null 2 permutes whole 100-micrometre spatial blocks within these strata, pairing blocks with cell counts within 10%, preserving source profiles and internal ordering; truncate neither unmatched blocks nor rare types to improve a result. Blocks without a valid match retain identity and are recorded. Recompute local contexts; do not permute receiver outcomes. Fit null models on correspondingly permuted training contexts and evaluate independently permuted held-out contexts using identical train-selected machinery.

Audit each null's changed-profile fraction (>=80% of eligible source cells) and ligand spatial autocorrelation (within 20% of observed at the primary neighbourhood scale, when estimable). Null 1 controls composition; only qualifying block nulls can satisfy the spatial-preservation gate. These are predeclared feasibility requirements, not guarantees. If no adequate null exists, report spatial-null validation NOT EVALUABLE and stop at exploratory association. Do not substitute arbitrary global shuffles. No patient, anatomy or cell-state labels are permuted across specimens.

## 8. Numerical gates, uncertainty and stopping

Retain >=5% patient-weighted held-out MSE reduction relative to **each** strong intrinsic/composition/anatomical/global-context baseline and the mean valid spatial-null model for >=2 independently supported programmes. Define improvement=(MSE_baseline - MSE_full)/MSE_baseline; an undefined/zero baseline loss is reported, not divided away. Resample whole patient pairs for 10,000 fixed-seed bootstrap replicates. Use paired patient loss differences, not independent cell errors. Report 95% intervals and all eight patient effects. Use a familywise programme correction (Holm test on exact patient sign-flip tests for the primary added-value contrast); bootstrap intervals describe magnitude, exact sign-flip symmetry assumptions must be stated. For confirmation require multiplicity-aware intervals via Bonferroni 98.333% intervals across three programmes excluding zero as well as 95% intervals. Eight patients can still give inconclusive uncertainty: NOT EVALUABLE, never automatic PASS.

Evaluate supported-context receivers and all eligible receivers separately. Added-value direction must agree in >=2/3 radii. Twenty repeats of 20% density/region-matched cell removal require same direction in >=80% and >=50% median original effect size. Repeat coverage and prediction at count floors 50/100/200; do not select the best floor. Context recomputation and baseline matching apply equally after downsampling. No new pathways, signatures, radii, strata or model classes may be added after outer-test inspection. Missing strata or underpowered effects remain explicit.

STOP before fitting if provenance/identity/dictionary/context/independent-anatomy gates fail or fewer than two programmes are supportable. STOP after the single prespecified model/null/sensitivity grid: no tuning around held-out results. A failed prediction supports inadequacy of **this measured snapshot and representation**, not a universal theorem that all static spatial transcriptomics fails. Persistent fibroblast states may remove present-context dependence, while unmeasured protein and history confound it. If adequate measurements yield no robust added value, move to direct physiological perturbation measurements with stimulation timing, baseline windows, repeated independent animals/cultures and measured responses. Do not attempt further arbitrary receptor-threshold changes.

Possible decisions: (i) blocked/NOT EVALUABLE for unavailable required inputs; (ii) NO-GO for this representation after measured gate or predictive failure; (iii) exploratory context association if only exploratory checks pass; (iv) validated context-response compatibility only after all new gates pass. None overwrites the original NO-GO or validates higher-level organisation automatically.
